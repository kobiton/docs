= Prepare iOS device for hosting
:navtitle: Prepare iOS device

Prepare an iOS or iPadOS device for hosting on Kobiton.

Settings and options may vary by device model and iOS or iPadOS version. Refer to Apple's documentation if an option is unavailable or appears in a different location.

== Requirements

* A supported xref:supported-platforms:mobile-operating-systems-and-devices.adoc[iOS or iPadOS device].
* A Mac with Xcode installed. You can use the Mac mini host where deviceConnect is installed.

== Factory reset the device

A factory reset is recommended for devices previously hosted on Legacy Kobiton or Legacy Gigafox.

[WARNING]
====
A factory reset erases all data on the device. Back up any data you need to retain before proceeding.
====

Follow Apple's instructions to link:https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/how-to-erase-all-content-and-settings-ips4603248a8/web[erase all content and settings,window=_blank].

== Turn on Developer Mode and UI Automation

Developer Mode and UI Automation must be enabled for Kobiton to control the device.

. Connect the device to a Mac with Xcode installed.
. Open Xcode.
. If prompted, trust the device in Finder and tap *Trust* on the iOS or iPadOS device.

Follow the steps for your device's OS version.

[tabs]
====

iOS 16 and later::
+
--

. On the device, open *Settings* and tap *Privacy & Security*.
. Under *Security*, tap *Developer Mode*.
. Turn on *Developer Mode*.

image:ios-settings-context-privacy-security.png[width=240,alt="Privacy & Security in Settings"] -> image:ios-privacy-security-context-developer-mode.png[width=240,alt="Developer Mode under Privacy & Security"] -> image:ios-developer-mode-context.png[width=240,alt="Developer Mode turned on"]

. Restart the device and confirm that you want to enable Developer Mode.
. Open *Settings* and tap *Developer*.
. Turn on *Enable UI Automation*.

image:ios-settings-context-developer.png[width=240,alt="Developer in Settings"] -> image:ios-deverloper-context-enable-ui-automation.png[width=240,alt="Enable UI Automation turned on"]

--

iOS 13 to 15::
+
--

. On the device, open *Settings* and tap *Developer*.
. Turn on *Enable UI Automation*.

image:ios-settings-context-developer-iphone-below-16.png[width=240,alt="Developer in Settings on iOS 13 to 15"] -> image:ios-developer-context-enable-ui-automation-iphone-below-16.png[width=240,alt="Enable UI Automation turned on in iOS 13 to 15"]

--

== Turn off automatic updates

Software updates make the device unavailable for testing until the updates are complete.

. Open *Settings* and tap *General* > *Software Update* > *Automatic Updates*.
. Turn off *Install iOS Updates*.

image:ios-software-update-context.png[width=240,alt="Automatic Updates on the Software Update screen"] -> image:ios-automatic-update-context.png[width=240,alt="Install iOS Updates turned off"]

. Return to *Settings* and tap *App Store*.
. Under *Automatic Downloads*, turn off *App Updates*.

image:ios-settings-context-app-store.png[width=240,alt="App Store in Settings"] -> image:ios-app-store-context-app-updates.png[width=240,alt="App Updates turned off"]

== Turn off the device passcode

A device passcode prevents Kobiton from controlling the device unless passcode mode is enabled for your organization.

To turn off an existing passcode:

. Open *Settings* and tap *Face ID & Passcode* or *Touch ID & Passcode*.
. Enter the current passcode.
. Tap *Turn Passcode Off*.

image:ios-settings-context-face-id-passcode.png[width=240,alt="Face ID & Passcode in Settings"] -> image:ios-enter-passcode-context.png[width=240,alt="Passcode entry screen"] -> image:ios-face-id-passcode-turn-passcode-off.png[width=240,alt="Turn Passcode Off option"]

. Enter the passcode again to confirm.

=== Using passcode mode

Contact Kobiton support to enable passcode mode for your organization.

In passcode mode, deviceConnect sets a passcode on devices supervised by your organization. Devices that use Bluetooth keyboard access do not require supervision for this feature.

If a device requires supervision but is not supervised by your organization, it fails its health check with the following error:

`Cannot set passcode: the device is not supervised by this organization.`

The device remains unavailable until it is xref:devices:local-devices/network-payload-capture/configure-an-ios-device.adoc#supervise_device[supervised] or passcode mode is disabled.

[#_turn_off_bringing_devices_together_required]
== Turn off Bringing Devices Together

Bringing Devices Together can interfere with manual testing sessions on iOS 17 and later.

. Open *Settings* and tap *General* > *AirDrop*.
. Turn off *Bringing Devices Together*.

image:device-lab-management:ios-prepare-general-airdrop.png[width=240,alt="AirDrop under General settings"] -> image:device-lab-management:ios-prepare-airdrop-bring-devices-together-off.png[width=240,alt="Bringing Devices Together turned off"]

== Enable automatic timezone updates

Automatic timezone updates must be enabled for the *Set device time zone* control to function during manual sessions.

. Open *Settings* and tap *Privacy & Security* > *Location Services* > *System Services*.
. Turn off *Setting Time Zone*.
. Return to *Settings* and tap *General* > *Date & Time*.
. Turn on *Set Automatically*.

== Turn on Web Inspector in Safari

Web Inspector and Remote Automation must be enabled to use Kobiton Inspector or web automation.

. Open *Settings* and tap *Safari*.
. Tap *Advanced*.
. Turn on *Web Inspector* and *Remote Automation*.

image:ios-settings-context-safari.png[width=240,alt="Safari in Settings"] -> image:ios-safari-context-advanced.png[width=240,alt="Advanced settings in Safari"] -> image:ios-avanced-context-web-inspector.png[width=240,alt="Web Inspector and Remote Automation turned on"]

== Turn off automatic brightness and Auto-Lock

Turning off automatic brightness lets Kobiton maintain minimum screen brightness to reduce battery consumption. Disabling Auto-Lock prevents the device from locking automatically during testing.

. Open *Settings* and tap *Display & Brightness*.
. Turn off *Automatic*.

image:ios-settings-context-display-brightness.png[width=240,alt="Display & Brightness in Settings"] -> image:ios-display-brighness-context-automatic.png[width=240,alt="Automatic brightness turned off"]

. Tap *Auto-Lock*.
. Select *Never*.

image:ios-display-brighness-context.png[width=240,alt="Auto-Lock under Display & Brightness"] -> image:ios-auto-lock-context.png[width=240,alt="Never selected for Auto-Lock"]
