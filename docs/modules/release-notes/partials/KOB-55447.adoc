== Tidy up lists offline simulators and emulators

Offline simulators and emulators now appear in the *Remove unused devices* window, opened from *Tidy up* in *Settings* > *Device Management*. Previously, a search for an offline simulator or emulator returned "No offline devices found."

* Offline simulators and emulators are listed alongside offline private devices.
* Select them and click *Confirm* to hide them from the device list.
* All listed devices are selected by default. If your organization has many offline simulators or emulators, review the selection before you confirm.
