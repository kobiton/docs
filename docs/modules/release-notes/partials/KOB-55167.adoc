== Fold and unfold foldable devices in manual sessions

Manual sessions on foldable Android devices, such as Galaxy Z Fold and Pixel Fold, now include a fold control in the session toolbar.

* The control appears above *Rotate Screen* on foldable devices and shows the action it performs next: *Fold device* or *Unfold device*.
* The session starts in the device's physical posture.
* After a fold or unfold, the device screen and *Inspector* switch to the size and shape of the active screen.
* Folds and unfolds are recorded as steps in Session Explorer.

Flip-style foldable devices, such as Galaxy Z Flip, are not supported.

For details, see xref:manual-testing:device-controls.adoc#_fold_or_unfold_device[Device controls].
