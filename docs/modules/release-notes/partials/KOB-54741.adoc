=== Portal

- Fixed an issue where opening Kobiton Portal at its root URL hid features included in your subscription until you refreshed the page. The *virtual devices* tab was missing from the device list, and Kobiton CLI packages were missing from Downloads in the left navigation. Opening a Portal page by its direct URL was not affected.
