== Foldable Android device support in manual sessions

Manual sessions on book-style foldable Android devices, such as Galaxy Z Fold, include *Fold device* and *Unfold device* controls. The device screen and *Inspector* switch to the active screen after each change, and Session Explorer records each fold and unfold as a timeline step with its own screenshot.

*Considerations*

* Fold and unfold controls are available in manual sessions only.
* Flip-style foldable devices, such as Galaxy Z Flip, are not supported.
* Host foldable devices with the hinge physically open. For details, see xref:device-lab-management:android-devices/prepare-android-device.adoc#host_foldable_devices[Prepare Android device for hosting].
* The device keeps its folded or unfolded posture from the previous session.

*Known issues*

* After a fold or unfold, the saved session video can appear stretched or distorted.
* In Session Explorer, *Inspector* can be misaligned on steps captured after a fold or unfold.

For more information, see xref:manual-testing:device-controls.adoc#_fold_or_unfold_device[Fold or unfold device].
