== Fold and unfold steps in Session Explorer

Session Explorer now shows when a foldable device was folded or unfolded during a manual session.

* Each fold or unfold appears on the timeline as a *FOLD DEVICE* or *UNFOLD DEVICE* step, with its own icon and screenshot.
* Screenshots keep the screen size and shape of the posture the device was in, instead of being stretched or cropped.
* Touch and swipe markers line up with the screenshot they belong to.

For details, see xref:session-explorer:analytics/use-the-session-timeline.adoc#_review_fold_and_unfold_steps[].
