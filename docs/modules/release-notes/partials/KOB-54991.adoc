== Device passcode

- In a manual session on a passcode-enabled iOS device, pressing *Power* to sleep the device and pressing it again to wake now auto-fills the passcode and returns the device to the Home screen, as documented. Previously, the device stayed on the lock screen and the session raised `Failed to unlock passcode!` about 15 to 18 seconds after the wake. While the device stayed locked, lockdown-backed operations, including application install, failed with `PasswordProtected` until a manual unlock or reboot.

_Applies to organizations in PIN passcode mode._
