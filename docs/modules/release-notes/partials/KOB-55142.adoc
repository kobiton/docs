== HarmonyOS devices recognized in the device list

HarmonyOS devices and emulators connected through deviceConnect are now recognized and listed correctly in Kobiton Portal.

* HarmonyOS devices and emulators show *HarmonyOS* as their platform and come online in the device list.
* The OS version shows the HarmonyOS version reported by the device, such as 6.1.0.117, instead of the device model.
* Real devices show their marketing name, such as HUAWEI Mate 80, instead of the model code.
* Ending a session on a HarmonyOS device no longer fails during post-session cleanup.

HarmonyOS devices that were connected before this release are updated the next time they reconnect.
