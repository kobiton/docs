== Lightning mode and session video recover after an iOS device reconnects

Fixed an issue in iOS manual sessions where, after the device's USB cable was unplugged and plugged back in, Lightning mode could not be turned back on without a page reload, and the session video showed the screen from before the unplug for about 2 minutes.

* After the device reconnects, the live view and the session video show the device's current screen right away.
* Lightning mode comes back without a page reload. If *Lightning Mode* is off after the device reconnects, turn it on to bring back the device's stream.
