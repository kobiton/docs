= Use Apple Pay on iOS devices
:navtitle: Use Apple Pay

Learn how to use Apple Pay to test payment flows in a Manual session on iOS devices.

NOTE: APPLE Pay testing is supported on Private and Local iOS devices only.

Before you start, xref:devices:local-devices/set-up-device-for-apple-pay.adoc[set up an iOS device for Apple Pay].

Log into an Apple ID account. Turn on *AssistiveTouch* if device cleanup policy cleaned it up.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-assistive-touch-settings-toggle.PNG[width=500,alt="The Assistive touch toggle turned on]

When an application or website requires Apple Pay confirmation, tap the *AssistiveTouch* icon on the screen.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-interactive-demo-payment.PNG[width=500,alt="The Payment screen with the Assistive touch icon"]

Tap *Device*.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-assistive-touch-device.PNG[width=500,alt="The Device option in Assistive touch expanded menu"]

Tap *More*.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-assistive-touch-more.PNG[width=500,alt="The More option in Assistive touch expanded menu"]

Tap *Pay*.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-assistive-touch-pay.PNG[width=500,alt="The Pay option in Assistive touch expanded menu"]

Tap *Confirm Pay*.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-assistive-touch-confirm-pay.PNG[width=500,alt="The Confirm Pay option in Assistive touch expanded menu"]

iOS displays the passcode screen as blank in the live stream. Enter the passcode with the passcode helper or with a keyboard.

== Enter the passcode with the passcode helper

The passcode helper shows the hidden passcode keypad as a tappable overlay on the device screen. The passcode helper is available in manual sessions only and supports iPhone screen sizes, portrait and landscape orientation, and Display Zoom.

. Click *Passcode helper*. The keypad overlay appears on the device screen.
. Click the digits of the device passcode on the overlay. Each tap enters the digit on the device. Entry progress shows as dots, and the digits are never displayed.
. After the first digit, *OK* appears on the overlay. After entering the full passcode, click *OK*.

The payment is confirmed.

Passcode digits are not displayed or saved in the session recording.

If a tap does not reach the device, the overlay shows an error message. Kobiton does not retry the tap automatically. Click the digit again.

== Enter the passcode with a keyboard

Tap the black space and enter the passcode using your keyboard. Contact the Kobiton Support, if needed, to retrieve the passcode set on the device.

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-enter-passcode-empty.PNG[width=500,alt="The passcode input screen"]

After inputting the whole passcode, press the *backspace* or *delete* key on your keyboard once, then input the last digit of the passcode. Tap the *Done* button when it is enabled:

image::bypass-apple-pay-manual-session-enter-passcode-empty.PNG[width=500,alt="The passcode input screen filled in"]

The payment is confirmed.
