= Add a card to Wallet for Apple Pay testing
:navtitle: Add a card to Wallet

During a manual iOS session, add an Apple Pay sandbox test card to Wallet to test payment flows without a real card.

NOTE: This procedure requires Apple Account sign-in, which is available on private and local iOS devices only.

== Requirements

* A xref:devices:local-devices/set-up-device-for-apple-pay.adoc[device set up for Apple Pay], with a passcode.
* A Sandbox Apple Account. Create one in App Store Connect. For details, see https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/sandbox-testing/[Apple Pay sandbox testing,window=_blank].
* Card details from Apple's sandbox test card list on the same page.

Sandbox test cards work only on a device signed in with a Sandbox Apple Account.

== Sign in with a Sandbox Apple Account

. xref:manual-testing:start-a-manual-session.adoc[Start a manual session] on the iOS device.
. If the device is signed in to another Apple Account, sign out first.
. Sign in with the Sandbox Apple Account. For steps, see xref:manual-testing:local-devices/sign-in-to-apple-account-in-manual-session.adoc[].

== Add a test card

. On the device screen, open *Wallet*.
. Tap *+* (Add).
. Tap *Debit or Credit Card*, then tap *Continue*.
. Tap *Enter Card Details Manually*.
. Enter the card number, name, expiration date, and security code of a test card from Apple's sandbox test card list.
. Follow the prompts on the device screen to accept the terms and finish adding the card.
. When the device asks for its passcode, enter it in the *Apple Security Enablement* dialog, then select *Submit*. For details, see xref:manual-testing:enter-ios-device-passcode.adoc[].

When the card appears in Wallet, the device is ready for Apple Pay testing. Continue with xref:manual-testing:local-devices/use-apple-pay-in-manual-session.adoc[].
