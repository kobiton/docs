= Foldable device considerations
:navtitle: Foldable devices

Use the xref:manual-testing:device-controls.adoc#_fold_or_unfold_device[fold control] to fold and unfold book-style foldable Android devices, such as Samsung Galaxy Z Fold and Google Pixel Fold, during a manual session. Flip-style devices, such as Samsung Galaxy Z Flip, are not supported.

The following limitations apply to Portal 2610.15 or later, deviceConnect 2610.15 or later, and Standalone 4.27.2S or later.

== Supported sessions

* Fold and unfold controls are available in manual sessions only.
* Session Explorer records fold and unfold steps for manual sessions only. When you convert a session to a test case, fold and unfold steps appear as unsupported steps.

== Fold position between sessions

The fold position does not reset when a session ends. The next session on the device starts in the position the previous session left it in, even if that position differs from the device's physical position. The fold control and the session stream always show the device's current position.

* To change the position for the current session, select *Fold device* or *Unfold device*. The next session starts in the position you leave the device in.
* To return a Private device to its physical position, xref:devices:manage-devices.adoc#_restart_private_device[restart the device].
* To return a Public device to its physical position, submit a support ticket to Kobiton to restart the device.

== Session video

After a fold or unfold, the session video can appear stretched or squeezed. The video frame size can change partway through the recording. When the frame shape does not match the device position, the video stretches or squeezes the screen to fill the frame.

No workaround is available. To view the screen at its correct size and shape, use the step screenshots in the xref:session-explorer:analytics/use-the-session-timeline.adoc#_review_fold_and_unfold_steps[Session Explorer timeline].

== Session Explorer

[#_inspector_alignment]
=== Inspector alignment

On some steps captured after a fold or unfold, *Inspector* highlights do not line up with the app's elements. Hovering over or selecting an element highlights a different area of the screenshot. Touch and swipe markers are not affected.

No workaround is available.

=== Step screenshots

The screenshot for a fold or unfold step is captured shortly after the change. If the device is slow to switch screens, the screenshot can still show the previous screen.

== Flip-style devices

Closing or opening a flip-style device during a session affects the session stream:

* *Closing the device*: The device turns off its main screen, so the stream goes black. The stream does not show the cover screen, and no workaround is available.
* *Opening the device again*: The stream stays black. To restore video, select *Power* to turn on the screen and show the lock screen, then select *Power* again to unlock the device. Alternatively, turn off *Lightning Mode* in *Speedometer*. With *Lightning Mode* off, video resumes at a lower frame rate.
* *Starting a session on a closed device*: The session shows only the cover screen's always-on display and cannot be used. *Unfold device* and *Home* have no effect.

== Hosting foldable devices

When hosting Private or Local foldable devices, such as Samsung Galaxy Z Fold and Samsung Galaxy Z Flip, keep them open. For details, see xref:device-lab-management:android-devices/prepare-android-device.adoc#_prepare_foldable_android_devices[Prepare foldable Android devices].
