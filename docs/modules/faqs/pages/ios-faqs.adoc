== Manual sessions

=== Why don't push notifications appear during an iOS manual session?

When an iOS device is connected to Kobiton, push notifications sent to the device may not appear on screen. iOS controls this through a Notifications setting for the Screen Sharing feature, which is off by default. This affects iOS 15 and later.

To display push notifications during a manual session, enable Notifications for Screen Sharing on the device:

. Open *Settings*.
. Select *Notifications*.
. Select *Screen Sharing*.
. Turn on *Allow Notifications*.
