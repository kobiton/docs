= Sign in with Enterprise SSO
:navtitle: Sign in with Enterprise SSO

Enterprise SSO lets users of participating organizations sign in to Kobiton Portal through their corporate identity provider (IdP). After the first successful sign-in, your email address is recognized on subsequent visits and the sign-in flow skips directly to the Enterprise SSO step.

== Prerequisites

* Your organization uses Enterprise SSO with Kobiton.
* Your organization administrator has provisioned your account.

== Sign in for the first time

. Open the https://portal.kobiton.com/login[Kobiton Portal sign-in page].
. Enter your corporate email address and select *Continue*.
. Select *Continue with Enterprise SSO*.
. Complete sign-in on your organization's IdP screen.
