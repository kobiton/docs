= Sign in with SSO
:navtitle: Sign in with SSO

If your organization uses Single Sign-On (SSO), you can sign in to Kobiton Portal through your organization's identity provider (IdP) instead of your Kobiton password.

== Prerequisites

* Your organization has SSO configured with Kobiton.
* Your administrator has either added your account to Kobiton Portal or added you to the IdP group that grants Kobiton access.

== Sign in for the first time

=== If your administrator created your Kobiton account

. Open the https://portal.kobiton.com/login[Kobiton Portal sign-in page].
. Enter your corporate email address and select *Continue*.
. Select *Continue with Enterprise SSO*.
. Complete sign-in on your organization's IdP screen.

On subsequent sign-ins, Kobiton Portal remembers your email address and routes you to your organization's IdP automatically.

=== If your administrator added you only through your IdP

Kobiton Portal does not yet have a record of your email address, so you identify your organization manually before Portal routes you to its IdP.

. Open the https://portal.kobiton.com/login[Kobiton Portal sign-in page].
. Below the email field, select *Join your company with SSO*.
. In the *Company name* field, enter your organization's SSO domain name. Ask your administrator if you don't know this value.
. Select *Continue with SSO*.
. Complete sign-in on your organization's IdP screen.
