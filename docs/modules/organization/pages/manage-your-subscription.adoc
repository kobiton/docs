= Manage your subscription
:navtitle: Manage your subscription

Use the Subscription section under *Account* to choose a plan, switch plans, review usage, cancel your self-serve subscription, and update billing information.

Managing a subscription requires the subscription-management permission. Org Owners hold this permission by default. ADMINs hold it only when granted. See xref:organization:roles/user-roles-and-permissions.adoc#_subscription_permissions[Subscriptions permissions] for details.

The Subscription section has three tabs:

* *Overview* shows your active plan, usage statistics, and the cancellation control.
* *My Plan* is where you purchase a plan or switch to a different one.
* *Billing Information* holds the payment and billing details tied to your account.

== Choose or change your plan

Purchasing a plan and switching plans use the same screen, whether you're starting a new paid plan or moving between tiers.

. In the left navigation, scroll down and select *Account*.
. Select *Subscription*, then select the *My Plan* tab.
. Select *Switch Plans*.
. Choose a billing cycle:
** *Monthly Plans* shows month-to-month pricing.
** *Annual Plans* shows annualized pricing.
. Pick a plan: *Startup*, *Accelerate*, *Scale*, or *Enterprise*.
** *Startup* is available on monthly billing only. On the annual view, Startup is marked *Monthly Only*.
** *Scale* is available on annual billing only. On the monthly view, Scale is marked *Annual Only*.
** *Enterprise* is not self-serve. Select *Contact Us* under the Enterprise tier to open a contact form, and a sales representative will follow up.
. Enter your credit card and billing details to complete the purchase.

== Billing information

The *Billing Information* tab shows the payment method and billing details tied to your subscription. Update any field in this tab to change what's on file.

. In the left navigation, scroll down and select *Account*.
. Select *Subscription*, then select the *Billing Information* tab.
. Update the fields you want to change, then save.

== Cancel your subscription

. In the left navigation, scroll down and select *Account*.
. Select *Subscription*. The *Overview* tab shows your active subscription details.
. Select *Cancel Subscription*.
. Review the end date shown in the confirmation message. Because the current billing cycle is already paid, you retain access until that date.
. Select *Yes, cancel my subscription* to confirm.

After cancellation, there is no reactivation option. To resume service, purchase a new plan from the *My Plan* tab.
