= Manage your subscription
:navtitle: Manage your subscription

Manage your subscription from *Account > Subscription*. You can choose or change a plan, review subscription usage, cancel your subscription, and update billing information.

Access to subscription management depends on your account permissions. Org Owners have access by default. ADMINs require additional permissions. See xref:users/user-roles-and-permissions.adoc[User roles and permissions].

== Choose or change a plan

. Select your profile picture, then select *Account > Subscription*.
. Under *My Plan*, select *Switch Plans*.
. Select *Monthly Plans* or *Annual Plans*.
. Select a plan.
. Enter your billing details to complete the purchase.

image::manage_subscription.png[width=800,alt="Manage your subscription"]

For Enterprise plans, select *Contact Us* to contact Kobiton.

== Cancel your subscription

Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation takes effect when that period ends.

. Select your profile picture, then select *Account > Subscription*.
. Under *My Plan*, select *Overview*.
. Select *Cancel Subscription*.
. Select *Yes, cancel my subscription* to confirm.

After cancellation, you can purchase a new plan from *My Plan*.

== Billing information

. Select your profile picture, then select *Account > Subscription*.
. Select *Billing Information*.
. Update your billing details and save your changes.
